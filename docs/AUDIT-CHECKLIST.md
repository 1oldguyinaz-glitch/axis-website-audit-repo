# Axis website growth audit checklist

Score each category from 1–10 and attach evidence. Separate facts from assumptions.

## 1. Five-second clarity
- Can a visitor identify the business, customer, outcome, and next action?
- Is the primary promise specific?
- Is the headline customer-centered?

## 2. Conversion path
- Is there one obvious primary CTA?
- Are CTAs visible at decision points?
- Does the path create unnecessary steps?

## 3. Trust and credibility
- Are claims supported by proof?
- Are testimonials, credentials, reviews, guarantees, and contact details credible?
- Are there fabricated or unverifiable claims? Remove them.

## 4. Mobile experience
- Readability, spacing, navigation, forms, tap targets, image cropping, and speed.

## 5. Offer and messaging
- Is the problem urgent and expensive enough?
- Is the outcome measurable?
- Are scope and exclusions clear?

## 6. Basic SEO and accessibility
- Unique title and description, one H1, image alt text, semantic headings, contrast, keyboard access, descriptive links.

## 7. Performance
- Oversized images, render-blocking assets, layout shifts, broken links, and unnecessary scripts.

## 8. Recommendation logic
Recommend the smallest complete solution. Explain why the lower tier under-solves the identified problems and why the higher tier would overbuild.

## Presentation output
1. Executive summary
2. Scorecard
3. Three highest-impact issues
4. Evidence screenshots
5. Quick wins
6. Recommended redesign plan
7. Fit-based package recommendation
8. Next step
