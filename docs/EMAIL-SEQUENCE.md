# Audit nurture sequence

The information is free. The paid offer is implementation, personalization, and time saved.

## Email 1 — Confirmation
**Subject:** Your Axis website audit request is in

Confirm receipt, set delivery expectations, and ask the recipient to reply with their primary website goal if they want it considered.

## Email 2 — Audit delivery
**Subject:** Your website audit is ready

Link or attach the audit. Summarize the three highest-impact issues. Do not pitch before delivering value.

## Email 3 — The cost of unclear messaging
Explain how unclear positioning causes qualified visitors to leave. Show one concrete fix.

## Email 4 — The conversion path
Teach the role of a clear CTA, proof, and reduced friction. Invite a reply with questions.

## Email 5 — Before-and-after breakdown
Show a real redesign or concept build. Explain each decision rather than only displaying aesthetics.

## Email 6 — Implementation options
Present the recommended scope first. Explain why smaller work would under-solve and larger work would overbuild.

## Email 7 — Direct decision
**Subject:** Should we fix this now, later, or leave it alone?

Offer three honest choices and one clear reply CTA. No fake scarcity.
