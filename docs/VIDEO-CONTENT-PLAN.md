# Website build video engine

Each build should generate one long-form video and 6–12 short clips.

## Repeatable episode structure
1. Show the original or fictional brief.
2. Identify three business problems.
3. Define the conversion goal.
4. Build the redesign on camera.
5. Explain headline, CTA, trust, layout, mobile, and color decisions.
6. Reveal before and after.
7. Invite viewers to request a free audit.

## First five builds
- Local contractor
- Restaurant
- Realtor
- Coach or consultant
- Medical or professional service

## Short-form hooks
- “This homepage looks fine, but it is losing leads for three reasons.”
- “I rebuilt this contractor website around one customer decision.”
- “The prettiest part of this redesign is not what increases conversion.”
- “Here is why I removed half the homepage.”
- “A $900 refresh would not fix this problem—and a $3,000 system would be overkill.”

## Proof rule
Do not claim increased conversions unless measured. Until real client data exists, label builds as concepts and discuss design rationale, not fabricated outcomes.
